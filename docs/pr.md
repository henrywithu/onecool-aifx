## 💡 LikenessAI R&D Plan: High-Fidelity Actor Likeness Model Architecture

This presentation outlines the R&D plan for a next-generation AI architecture designed to solve critical limitations in our actor likeness generation system, moving us from a limited prototype to a **production-ready, high-fidelity solution**.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Problem

We currently face four critical challenges that prevent us from delivering production-quality, convincing digital actors.

Our **Current Status** is a prototype limited to 5-second videos capturing only basic movements. Our **Target** is a production-ready system capable of generating high-fidelity, emotionally diverse, full-body actor representations.

### The Four Core Problems

| Category | Specific Issue | Impact |
|:---------|:---------------|:-------|
| **Data Ingestion** | Limited, short video data, insufficient for nuanced generation. |
| **Facial Consistency** | Likeness is inconsistent across clips, leading to high visual artifact rates. |
| **Emotional Range** | Model "hallucinates" non-likeness faces when prompted for unlearned emotions. |
| **Holistic Realism** | System focuses only on the face, ignoring full physique, posture, and signature motor traits. |

---

## 🧠 Technical Architecture Overview

To solve these problems, we are introducing a five-layer pipeline built on Google's **Genkit** orchestration framework and **Veo 3.0** for core generation.

The flow proceeds logically: **Data Capture** feeds into **Quality Validation** and **Feature Extraction**. The extracted features form the **Identity Embedding**, which is checked by the **Consistency Validator**. Gaps in emotional data are filled by the **Synthetic Generation** loop, feeding into **Veo 3.0 Generation** and **Likeness Refinement**, before achieving the **Final Output**.

---

## 🛠️ Challenge 1: Data Ingestion Enhancement

### The Solution: Multi-Modal Data Ingestion Pipeline

Our current data is too sparse. We are implementing a **Multi-Modal Data Ingestion Pipeline** to ensure quality, diversity, and depth.

The core of this is the **Data Quality Validator**, a TypeScript interface that automatically scores incoming video based on five key dimensions: Resolution, Lighting, Face Visibility, Motion Blur, and Diversity. Clips must score above a **0.7 threshold** to be accepted for training.

This is supported by an **Enhanced Data Collection Protocol**:

| Data Type | Duration | Requirements | Purpose |
|:----------|:---------|:-------------|:--------|
| **Neutral Reference** | 30-60s | Multi-angle, consistent lighting | Identity baseline |
| **Expression Range** | 5-10s each | 8+ emotions, varying intensity | Emotion modeling |
| **Full-Body Movement** | 30-60s | Walking, gesturing | Motor trait capture |

This will lead to a **10x increase in training data diversity** and comprehensive feature representation.

## 🔒 Challenge 2: Facial Consistency Enforcement

### The Solution: Identity Embedding & Consistency Validation

To stop the likeness from drifting, we are introducing a mechanism to preserve identity across all generations.

We use a **768-dimensional Identity Embedding** vector, generated by embedding detailed facial descriptions from Gemini Vision (due to current multimodal API limitations). This vector acts as the actor's *canonical likeness*.

The **Consistency Validator** then checks every generated clip:

$$similarity = \frac{(A \cdot B)}{(\lVert A \rVert \times \lVert B \rVert)}$$

It calculates the **Cosine Similarity** between the generated face's description embedding (*B*) and the canonical Identity Embedding (*A*). We will **automatically reject** any generation where the score is below the strict threshold of **0.85**.

The outcome is a **95%+ consistency rate** and quantifiable likeness metrics for quality assurance.

## 😮 Challenge 3: Emotional Range Expansion

### The Solution: Synthetic Emotion Generation with Veo 3.0

The problem of the model "hallucinating" non-likeness faces for unlearned emotions is solved by generating the missing data synthetically.

The **Missing Emotion Generator** first identifies gaps using the `SuitabilityReport`. It then uses **Veo 3.0** to generate high-quality, 8-second video clips of the actor expressing the target emotion (e.g., ecstatic, weary) at specified intensities.

Crucially, every synthetic clip generated is **validated for consistency** against the Identity Embedding (Challenge 2) and retried if the score is below 0.85.

This process will create a vast emotional library covering **30+ distinct emotions** across Subtle, Moderate, and Intense levels, ultimately **eliminating face hallucination** for known emotional states.

---

## 🚶 Challenge 4: Holistic Realism Integration

### The Solution: Full-Body Multi-Modal Modeling

We must move beyond the face to capture the *full* actor.

We are expanding our data capture to include full-body references, detailed physique descriptions, and signature **Motor Traits**—like a specific walking gait or hand gesture.

This is supported by the **Likeness Parameter Refinement** flow, which allows users to fine-tune the output using natural language instructions, such as: *"Modify walking gait to include slight limp on left leg"* or *"Adjust posture to be more relaxed."*

Finally, a **Multi-Dimensional Validation** step ensures the generated content is accurate across all four pillars: Facial Consistency, Body Proportions, Motor Trait Fidelity, and Contextual Accuracy.
